Our story, baked from scratch

From a home kitchen to a neighbourhood favourite.

Our History

Sweet Harvest Bakery was started in 2019 by Thandi Nkosi, who began baking bread and cakes from her home kitchen for friends and neighbours in Melville, Johannesburg. Word spread quickly, and within a year Thandi opened a small shopfront selling artisan bread, cakes and pastries to the local community.

Today, Sweet Harvest Bakery is a well known part of the Melville community, known for its warm atmosphere and quality ingredients.

Our Mission

To bring wholesome, handmade baked goods to every table using quality local ingredients.

Our Vision

To be the neighbourhood's favourite bakery, known for freshness, warmth and community involvement.

What guides our baking

  • Quality first. We use fresh, local ingredients and traditional baking methods.
  • Community at heart. We are proud to be part of Melville and support local events.
  • Made fresh, daily. Everything is baked in small batches every morning.
  • Personal touch. Every custom cake is designed around your celebration.

Meet the Team

Sweet Harvest Bakery is run by founder and head baker Thandi Nkosi, along with a small team of pastry chefs and front of house staff who all share the same love of good, honest baking.

We are always happy to chat about your next order, or just to talk about bread. Pop in and say hello, or get in touch online.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I order a cake?

We recommend contacting us at least one week before your event. Larger or detailed orders may need more notice.

Yes. Use our enquiry form to tell us the flavour, design, servings and date you have in mind.
